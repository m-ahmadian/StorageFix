StorageFix 3.1 — Sandboxed Store Edition (local test build)
Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel included.

1. Drag StorageFix into Applications and launch it.
2. Click Scan My Mac, then Allow (the panel opens on your Home folder). macOS
   asks only once. Caches are measured first, then your Downloads folder.
   Cleanup lists every category; caches are deleted, installers and leftovers
   of deleted apps go to Trash. Choose Folder… reviews any other folder.
3. Duplicates: compare full paths, choose a copy to keep, select extra copies,
   then confirm Trash. At least one copy stays; changed files require a rescan.
4. Large & old files: size/type/modified-date/path filters, explicit selections,
   then Review Selected. The sheet includes selections hidden by filters.
5. Storage map: Explore, Back, Root, Show in Finder. Read-only exploration.
6. Applications: Choose Folder, select Applications and Scan. Inventory is
   read-only in this edition; Show opens Finder. App Store Updates opens Apple’s
   App Store. This edition does not uninstall apps or touch Apple system caches.
7. Safety checklist records your own review. Open Settings, search for the
   setting indicated, then mark Reviewed. No security settings are modified.
8. After a completed Quick Review, View Report shows each area's scope, counts,
   sizes and errors. Export Report saves a dated text snapshot where you choose.
   Figures overlap and are not a total of reclaimable space.
9. Privacy Policy is available from Help. Clear History removes local activity.

Access is limited to folders you allow. Only the Home permission is remembered;
Forget Cache Access in the app menu removes it.
No administrator privileges, Full Disk Access, network access or background
agent is requested. A denied/inaccessible folder reports an error.

Testing Samples
Copy the DMG’s Testing Samples into Downloads, then choose the copied root.
Exports contains two identical 12 MB files; Projects contains a unique third.
For Large & old files, select 10 MB+, Other and Any age. Three files appear.
Explore Exports on the map and Back. Review a selection and Cancel; if you
confirm on these disposable samples, restore the file from Finder Trash.
Scanned sizes exclude hidden files, bundles, empty files, symlinks and hard links.
APFS sharing can make logical file sizes differ from physical disk usage.

This local sandbox test app is ad hoc signed and has not been App Store reviewed.
It cannot be submitted without the publisher's distribution signing identity,
App Store Connect metadata and a publicly hosted privacy policy/support contact.
